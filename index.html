I know the distance between Bengaluru and Kolkata can make even the simplest things feel far away. And if I am lucky enough to call you my wife, I want to celebrate the day you were born every single year. Not just with gifts, but by making you feel cherished in the little everyday ways. That's what I want being Huzaif's wife to mean.

A letter for my Zuu.

To my Zinat,

Happy birthday, my baby. Happy birthday, my Zuu. ♡ I still find it beautiful that fate brought us into each other's lives from nowhere in particular. Out of all the people, all the places and all the possible stories, somehow ours began. I don't know how the universe arranged that little coincidence, but I am grateful for the chapter it gave me. I hope I can be a place where your heart gets to rest — a little relief after a long day, a safe corner in a noisy world, someone with whom you never have to pretend to be okay. I want us to laugh ridiculously, learn each other patiently, and face life as a team. Not you against the world, and not me against the world: us, hand in hand, figuring it out together. The miles between Bengaluru and Kolkata are real, and I wish they weren't. But I don't want distance to be the only thing our story remembers. I'll keep putting in the work to become more capable, more steady and more prepared for the life I dream of sharing with you. I want to build something worthy of your hopes — a life where you feel supported, respected, free and deeply loved. If I get the privilege of calling you my wife one day, I want to celebrate your birthday every year. I'll celebrate the day you arrived in this world because loving you should mean being delighted that you exist — not just on birthdays, but on ordinary Tuesdays too. I'll bring the hibiscus, the chocolates, the ice cream, the silly surprises and the hugs I owe you. I want to make room for the child in you who deserves to play, be curious, be spoiled a little, laugh without holding back and feel safe enough to be soft. You don't have to carry everything all the time. You get to be my baby, my Zuu, and still be the strong, brilliant, wonderfully unpredictable person you are. And whatever the future writes, your happiness matters. I hope life is gentle with you. I hope your dreams find you. I hope you keep discovering reasons to smile. I would love to be beside you for all of it — and I will keep hoping, working and choosing kindness along the way. For today, here's a forehead kiss from far away, a whole garden of hibiscus, and every birthday wish I can fit between these pages. Happy birthday, my love. May this year be soft on your heart and generous to your dreams. Always cheering for you, Huzaif ♡
H♡Z
